2. Overview

Site Reports ("App", "we", "us") provides on-device tools for:

  • Documenting site issues with photos
  • Annotating photos (arrows, boxes, drawings, text notes)
  • Optional GPS and time stamps on photos and reports
  • Generating branded PDF reports with your logo and signature

We do not upload your projects, photos or reports to servers operated by us. Core content processing occurs locally on your device, except when you intentionally export or share content through a service you select.

We operate no application servers and do not integrate analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs into the App. Apple may separately provide us with aggregated App Store analytics, sales information, subscription information, or diagnostic data under Apple's own privacy practices and your device settings. We do not intentionally collect personal information, except when you voluntarily contact us by email or when Apple provides the purchase information necessary to verify your subscription entitlement.

This Privacy Policy applies only to the App itself. It does not apply to Apple, the App Store, or third-party services that you choose to use when sharing exported reports.

3. Data We Process

3.1 On-Device Content (No Uploading)

All projects, issues, photos, annotations, notes, company details, logos and signatures you create are processed and stored locally on your device using Apple frameworks (SwiftUI, SwiftData, Core Graphics, PDFKit). The App does not automatically upload this content to servers operated by us. Your content leaves your device only when you choose to share or export it, or when it is included in device backups managed under your Apple settings.

3.2 Camera Access (Optional)

Camera access is requested solely so you can photograph site issues. Photos captured or imported for a project are stored within the App's local storage on your device. You may deny camera permission and, where supported, select existing photos using Apple's system photo picker instead.

3.3 Location Access (Optional)

Location access is requested solely to add optional GPS stamps to your photos and reports for documentation purposes. Location data is:

  • Processed entirely on your device
  • Embedded only in the photos and PDF reports you choose to create

The App itself never transmits your location to us or to third parties. If you share a report containing GPS stamps, that information travels only through the channel you choose (see Section 5.3).

You may deny or revoke this permission at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services. The App can still be used without location permission, but GPS-stamping features will be unavailable.

3.4 In-App Purchases (Handled by Apple)

Payments are processed exclusively by Apple. We do NOT receive:

  • Credit card or payment information
  • Your Apple ID, name, email, phone number
  • Billing address

We only receive the purchase information necessary to verify your subscription entitlement and unlock Pro features. We do not receive your payment information or Apple ID credentials. Apple's privacy policy applies to payments: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

3.5 Optional Diagnostic Data

Apple may provide us with crash reports, performance information or aggregated usage metrics when users have enabled the relevant sharing settings ("Share with App Developers"). We use this information solely to diagnose technical problems and improve the App. We do not use it for advertising or cross-app tracking. Apple's privacy practices apply to Apple's collection and processing of this information. Disable anytime: Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

3.6 Contacting Support

If you email us, we receive your email address and message contents. This information is used only to respond to your request. We retain support communications only for as long as necessary to handle your request and to keep reasonable records of our correspondence. You may request deletion at any time.

Except for support communications, purchase verification information and Apple-provided diagnostics described in this Policy, we do not intentionally collect, store, track or access:

  • Photos, annotations or exported reports

We do NOT sell, share, or rent any data to third parties.

5. How Your Content Is Handled

5.1 Local Only

Your content remains on your device; we do not upload it or store it externally. If you delete the App, its local data is deleted with it. We cannot access or recover deleted content. Copies included in your device backups (Section 5.2) or reports you have already shared (Section 5.3) are under your control, not ours.

5.2 Device Backups

Your App content may be included in your personal device backups (e.g. iCloud device backup) under Apple's terms. You control backups from your device settings; we have no access to them.

5.3 Shared Reports

When you export or share a PDF report, it is delivered only to the destination you choose (email, messaging apps, cloud drives, etc.). We do not keep, access or track copies. Content you share is governed by the privacy practices of the services you share it with, and by your own obligations toward your clients.

Reports you export may contain personal information, photographs, GPS coordinates or other information depending on the options you selected. You are solely responsible for reviewing exported reports before sharing them.
