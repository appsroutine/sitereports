Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Site Reports ("the app") is designed around a simple principle: your data belongs to you and stays on your device.

Data we collect

None. Site Reports does not collect, transmit, store or share any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Where your content is stored

All projects, issues, photos, notes, logos and signatures you create are stored locally on your device only. They are never uploaded to us or to any third party. If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it. Your content may be included in your personal device backups (e.g. iCloud device backup) under Apple's terms, which you control from your device settings.

Camera access

The app requests camera access solely so you can photograph site issues. Photos are saved inside the app on your device.

Location access

The app requests location access solely to add optional GPS stamps to your photos and reports for documentation integrity. Location data is processed on the device and embedded only in the reports you choose to generate. It is never sent to us.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment information. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store account settings. The terms of use are Apple's standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement (EULA).

Reports you share

When you export or share a PDF report, you decide where it goes (email, messaging apps, cloud drives, etc.). Content of shared reports is under your control and is governed by the services you share it with.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for professional use and is not directed at children under 13. Since the app collects no data, no personal information from children is processed.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the app handles data, we will update this page and note it in the app's release notes before the change takes effect.
